OUR STORY

3KG is a hairstyling brand built on creativity, confidence, and self-expression. Known for delivering clean cuts, modern styles, and quality service, 3KG continues to grow as a trusted name for clients who want to stand out and feel confident in their look. The brand represents more than just hairstyling — it represents passion, originality, and the culture of expressing yourself through style. Every service is focused on giving clients a professional experience while bringing out their best and most confident version.

Behind 3KG is D-k, a dedicated hairstyling artist with a passion for transforming ideas into unique and stylish looks. Through his creativity, precision, and commitment to his craft, D-k has built 3KG into a brand that values both quality and individuality. His vision is to create a space where clients can feel comfortable, confident, and truly express themselves through every haircut and style.
